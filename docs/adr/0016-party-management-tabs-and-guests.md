# ADR 0016: Party management in tabs, guest management

Status: accepted (2026-10-20)

## Context
Party management (members, invitations) and name editing stood side by side as a detail with an "Upravit partu" (Edit party) button. Guest management (players without an account) was added and the navigation needed to be thought through.

## Decision
- **Tabs as separate pages** (`PartyHeader`): the header "Parta <name>" (Party <name>) and below it a switcher **Členové** (Members) (`/parties/:id`) · **Hosté** (Guests) (`/parties/:id/guests`) · **Nastavení** (Settings) (`/parties/:id/edit`, admin only). Each section has its own address (link, history, switching the party in the menu keeps the section). The secondary button on all tabs is "Zrušit" (Cancel) and closes the whole party management (leads to the game list). The "Upravit partu" button is gone, replaced by the Settings tab. I did not choose single-page collapsible sections: a long page with several forms would have several Save buttons at once, and the main buttons (ADR 0015) are one bar per page.
- **Guests** (`PartyGuestsPage`, accessible to every party member): a list of players without an account with the number of plays; names are edited directly in the list and saved in bulk with the Uložit (Save) button (only changed ones), a new guest is added with a field at the bottom (Enter or Přidat (Add)), only a guest without plays can be deleted.
- **Backend:** `GET /parties/{id}/players` returns `playCount`; `DELETE /players/{id}` deletes only a guest who has not played anywhere and is not a player of any game; renaming a player linked to an account is rejected (their name follows the nickname); names consisting only of whitespace are rejected. A member who leaves the party or is removed becomes a guest (plays remain), so they can be renamed in Guests.
- **Showing guests:** a guest has a `visible` flag (`player.visible`, on by default, migration `Version20261022000000`; `PATCH /players/{id}` takes `name` and/or `visible`). A hidden guest is not offered in the play form (not even after "Přidat hosty" (Add guests)), but stays visible in a play in which they already play (play editing, players preselected from the session's last play); old plays and statistics do not change. The toggle is on every guest in Guests and is saved together with the name by the Save button.
- **Confirmation in a popup:** removing a member, leaving the party and deleting a guest are confirmed in a popup (`ConfirmPopup`, the same base and look as the select: page overlay, cross in the place of the party button), not the native `confirm`. The popup explains what will happen (a member becomes a guest, plays remain; a deleted guest disappears completely), the default focus is on "Zrušit" (Cancel), and after closing focus returns to the original button. Deleting a game in the game form still uses the native `confirm` for now.
